Module 01 · Torts and Tortious Liability Introduction

Nature

Law of Torts & Consumer Protection · Topic 1.2

1. Nature of Tort
1. Tort is a Civil Wrong

Tort is a civil wrong that generally affects the private rights of an individual. It is different from a crime, which is considered a wrong against society or the State.

2. Branch of Civil Law

Tort is an important branch of civil law that deals with the protection of individual rights and provides remedies to injured persons.

3. Duty Imposed by Law

In tort, the duty is imposed by law and not created by an agreement between the parties. Every person must respect the legal rights of others.

4. Remedy for the Injured Person

The main purpose of tort law is to provide relief to the injured person. The usual remedy is unliquidated damages, which means compensation whose amount is decided by the court. An injunction may also be granted in appropriate cases.

5. Protection of Legal Rights

Tort law protects the rights of individuals relating to their body, property, reputation and personal liberty. In certain cases, violation of a legal right is sufficient even without actual financial loss.

6. Independent of Contract

Tortious liability does not depend on any prior agreement. It arises from the breach of a legal duty imposed by law.
